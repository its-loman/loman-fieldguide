I don't want this site to behave like a résumé with better typography.

I want it to feel like a place where somebody has been **looking at things carefully** and keeping the useful pieces.

That's why I'm calling it a field guide.

A field guide doesn't need to know everything. It records what was observed, what seemed important, and enough context to recognize the thing again later.

## Working notes are allowed to be wrong

One of the reasons I like this framing is that a note can be provisional.

I can write down what I currently think about rendering, frameworks, performance, or developer tooling without pretending I've carved it into stone.

If the idea changes, the note changes.

That's healthier than turning every thought into a declaration.

## What belongs here

Mostly things about the web:

- architecture and rendering
- performance
- frameworks and their internals
- developer tooling
- experiments that teach me something
- small implementation details I know I'll forget

And occasionally whatever else survives the walk back to the notebook.
